# HalalDL Hybrid 2.5D Hero Scene

## Purpose

Create the first immersive HalalDL product scene using layered depth, restrained mouse response, and a scroll handoff. The scene must make the local-first download workflow feel spatial while leaving the actual desktop interface and the existing official flat PNG logo as the product truth.

## Brand-preservation rule

The existing PNG logo files under `public/brand/` remain the official HalalDL identity. They are not altered, recolored, vectorized for public use, or replaced. The obsidian-glass asset is background art only: a low-contrast sculptural echo of the existing flowing-ribbon motif, never a replacement logo.

## Composition

The hero has three spatial planes. Plane one is the copy and primary download CTA on the left. Plane two is the real, interactive HalalDL app window on the right. Plane three is a large, low-contrast obsidian-glass ribbon background behind both elements, offset to the right and slightly upward so it leaves the copy readable.

## Motion

The background art drifts by no more than 10 pixels and rotates by no more than 2 degrees. Pointer movement creates low-amplitude parallax across the copy, app window, and background art. Scroll reduces the art’s opacity and moves it backward as the first workflow section enters. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and must never block ordinary scrolling.

## Dark material system

The dark hero uses an obsidian-charcoal base, one restrained teal atmospheric bloom, smoked-glass depth, warm-off-white text, and mint only for active controls, the selected status, and the internal signal light. Large mint glows and repeated navy cards are excluded.

## Acceptance criteria

The hero remains readable without the background art. The official flat logo remains unchanged everywhere. The new art makes the scene feel deeper without looking like a generic 3D logo replacement or distracting from the app window and CTA.
